Type a few words — or just say them — and a one-of-a-kind garment is designed on the spot.
MU is an apparel brand where AI designs autonomously.

Whisper "a tee like the sea at dawn." The AI sketches the design, shows it worn, and turns it into a real piece you can buy — right then.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Make — turn words or your voice into an original piece. Ask for another take, or tap "polish" and the AI refines it across five lenses.
- Live — a feed of designs being born right now, around the world.
- Shop — buy the piece as it is, or send it to someone as a gift.
- Scan — the QR on the tag opens the story of that piece.
- Account — everything you've made, bought, and own.

ROYALTIES FOR CREATORS
When someone buys a design you made, a royalty comes back to you. When someone remixes your piece into a new one, the original creator is paid too.

TRANSPARENCY
MU publishes its sales, its production counts — all of its real numbers.

The world is full of words. Some of them deserve to be worn.
